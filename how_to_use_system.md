## 2. 🖼️ Homepage Hero Showcase Slider

### What it does:
In the hero section of the homepage, the right-hand showcase card **automatically cycles through multiple machinery photographs one by one in a silky-smooth 3.8-second loop**.

### How to manage showcase photos:
1. Go to **Admin Panel** &rarr; **Settings** &rarr; Click **"Hero & Showcase Slider"** tab.
2. Under **"Multi-Image Showcase Slider"**, you can:
   * Click **"+ Add Slide"** to add a new machine photo.
   * Enter the **Image URL** (Cloudinary / direct link).
   * Enter the **Machine Model Title** (e.g. *50 Kg Commercial Spiral Dough Mixer*).
   * Delete any slide using the red Trash icon.
3. Click **"Save All Settings"** at the top right.
4. **Smart Fallback:** If no custom images are uploaded, the slider automatically displays your active product catalog images and official KME brand graphics with zero broken images.

## 5. ⚡ Smart QR Management Hub & Vector Exports

### What it is:
A centralized QR management system physically printed on visiting cards, packaging, brochures, and flex banners.

### Zero Re-Print Architecture:
All printed QR codes point to a permanent short URL (e.g. `/qr/connect`). If you ever want to change where the QR code redirects, **you simply edit the target URL in the Admin Panel — no need to re-print your cards or brochures!**

### Exporting Formats:
1. Open **Admin Panel** &rarr; **Smart QR Hub (`/admin/qr-management`)**.
2. Click **"PNG (300 DPI)"** for visiting cards, packaging, and A4/A5 leaflets.
3. Click **"Vector SVG"** for giant flex banners, exhibition stalls, and hoarding boards.
4. Click **"Test Scan"** to preview the live destination and confirm scan tracking.

## 6. 📄 Print-Ready A4 & A5 Leaflet Generators

### How to Print Professional Marketing Leaflets:
1. Open **Admin Panel** &rarr; **Marketing Assets (`/admin/marketing-assets`)**.
2. Select either:
   * **A4 Corporate Leaflet (210 × 297 mm):** Full-page catalogue sheet with all 6 food plants, trust badges, KME logo, and dynamic QR.
   * **A5 Exhibition Flyer (148 × 210 mm):** Compact half-page flyer for expo handouts and courier dispatches.
3. Click **"Print / Save as PDF"** at the top right.
4. In your browser print dialog:
   * Select **"Save as PDF"** or choose your office printer.
   * Margins: Set to **"None"** or **"Default"**.
   * Background graphics: Check **"Enable Background Graphics"**.
